The Defang Playground has been shut down, and you can no longer deploy to it. Defang Deploy runs your app in your own AWS, Google Cloud or Azure account. See [Getting Started](/docs/intro/getting-started), or [migrate an existing project to your own cloud account](/docs/tutorials/deploy-to-your-cloud).
