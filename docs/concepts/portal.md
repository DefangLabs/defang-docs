# Portal

You can log into the Defang Portal at [portal.defang.io](https://portal.defang.io) using your [Defang account](https://docs.defang.io/docs/concepts/accounts.md) to view the state of your Defang services.

You can use the Portal to get an overview of your [services](https://docs.defang.io/docs/concepts/services.md), view the logs for each service, quickly access exposed ports, view environment variables, and more. The Portal is also where you manage your [workspaces](https://docs.defang.io/docs/concepts/workspaces.md), invite team members, and configure billing.

info

To view services deployed to Defang BYOC, please check out [Monitoring Your Services](https://docs.defang.io/docs/tutorials/monitoring-your-services.md).

tip

Need help with a failing deployment? Defang provides a tool to help [debug](https://docs.defang.io/docs/concepts/debug.md) in your application.
